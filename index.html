Privacy Policy

Food Manager Exam Prep · Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Food Manager Exam Prep mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Service. We take your privacy seriously.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information you provide directly

  • Subscription purchase data — processed by Apple App Store via StoreKit. We do not receive your Apple ID or payment card details. Apple provides only a transaction token and an anonymous subscription identifier.
  • Feedback or support messages — if you contact us by email, we keep your message and reply address to help you.

b. Information stored locally on your device

The App stores your study progress, bookmarked questions, exam scores, daily streak, and in-app unlock status in UserDefaults and local JSON files on your device. This data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly export it or back up your device to iCloud/iTunes.

c. Information we do not collect

  • No real name, phone number, or physical address
  • No precise or coarse geolocation
  • No contacts, photos, or media files
  • No IMEI, IDFA, or persistent device identifier used for tracking
  • No keystroke data or text content you type into the App
Summary: Food Manager Exam Prep is a study tool. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell data.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver the App's core features: practice sessions, simulated exams, progress tracking, streaks, and badges
  • To verify premium purchase entitlement through Apple StoreKit 2 (non-renewing access)
  • To respond to support or feedback inquiries you send us
  • To detect bugs and crashes using Apple's native crash-reporting mechanisms (no third-party analytics SDK by default)

3. Third Parties

We rely on Apple for the following services. Please review Apple's own privacy policies for more details:

  • Apple StoreKit — subscription and one-time purchase processing. Apple Privacy Policy
  • App Store Connect — aggregated, anonymous sales and crash analytics. App Analytics

We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or any other third-party analytics or advertising SDK into the App.

4. Non-Renewing Access & Purchases

All Pro access is non-renewing. You pay once for a fixed period (1 month, 3 months, or 1 year) and access expires automatically at the end of that period — you will not be charged again unless you buy another plan. All payments are handled by Apple through StoreKit. When you purchase a monthly, 3-month, or yearly non-renewing plan, Apple processes the payment and returns a transaction identifier. We store only this identifier and the resulting unlock state (premium vs. free) locally on your device and in Apple's signed transaction records. We never see your credit card number, CVV, or billing address.

5. Data Retention

Local data (progress, bookmarks, exam history) stays on your device until you reset it from within the App or delete the App. We do not maintain our own server database of user study activity.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult learners preparing for food-safety certification exams. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you through an in-app message or update description.
