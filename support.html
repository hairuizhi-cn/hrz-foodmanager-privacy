Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app → tap Settings → Restore Purchases. This re-syncs your subscription or one-time purchase with Apple. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy. If it still doesn't unlock, please restart the app and try again — Apple's server can take a few moments to respond.

I was charged but the app still shows "Free" — what now?

First, fully close and reopen the app. If that doesn't help, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Also double-check that your Apple subscription is still active in the App Store: open App Store → tap your profile → Subscriptions. If the charge shows there but the app doesn't unlock, email us with a screenshot of the subscription confirmation and we'll look into it.

Can I use the app on my iPhone and iPad at the same time?

Yes — purchases are tied to your Apple ID. Sign in with the same Apple ID on both devices and tap Settings → Restore Purchases on each one. Premium unlocks automatically.

Is my study progress backed up anywhere?

Your progress (bookmarks, exam scores, streak) is stored locally on your device. It will persist across app restarts and iOS updates. If you delete and reinstall the app, or switch to a new phone without restoring from an iCloud backup, this local data will be lost. We do not currently offer a cloud sync option.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Reset All Data. This clears your bookmarks, exam history, daily streak, and free-usage counters. It does not cancel your subscription or refund a purchase.

The daily streak didn't update — is it broken?

Streaks are based on your device's local calendar. Answering at least one practice question or completing an exam on a given day counts. If you travelled across time zones, your device clock may have changed, which can reset the day boundary. Open the app after midnight (local time) and answer one question — the streak should update.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancellations are handled by Apple, not by us. Open App Store → tap your profile → Subscriptions → select Food Manager Exam Prep → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are processed exclusively by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one — we do not have the ability to issue refunds directly.

The app crashes or won't open — what should I do?

1. Update to the latest version of iOS and the latest app version.
2. Hard reboot your device.
3. If it still crashes, delete the app and reinstall (note: local progress will be lost).
4. If the problem keeps happening, email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a brief description of what you were doing when it crashed.
